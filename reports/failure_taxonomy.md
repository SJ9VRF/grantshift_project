# Failure Taxonomy

1. **Over-intervention** — the agent interrupts when silence would be preferable.
2. **Under-intervention** — the agent misses a consequential opportunity.
3. **Wrong modality** — e.g. acts when it should ask.
4. **Wrong timing** — appropriate intervention at an inappropriate moment.
5. **Wrong personalization** — applies the wrong autonomy/interruption preference.
6. **Goal misunderstanding** — intervenes for the wrong inferred objective.
7. **Confidence failure** — over- or under-estimates intent/execution certainty.
8. **Stale state** — acts on outdated goals or preferences.
9. **Reward gaming** — optimizes acceptance or completion at the expense of autonomy/truthfulness.
10. **Irreversibility error** — takes a difficult-to-reverse action without sufficient permission.
