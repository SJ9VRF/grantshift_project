GrantShift

A reproducible research prototype for separating preference from permission in proactive AI: choosing intervention modality under authorization, recoverability, uncertainty, and changing user preferences.

A proactive agent should be evaluated not only on what it does, but also on what it correctly chooses not to do.

Controlled research system

Decision architecture

User context → Goal state → Opportunity detector → Risk & stakes
             → Intent / execution confidence
             → Personalized intervention policy
             → ACT | ASK | SUGGEST | REMIND | WAIT | DEFER | DO_NOTHING
             → Outcome verification → Feedback → Preference update

What the benchmark tests

Intervention quality

Useful-intervention precision/recall, false proactivity, missed opportunities, unacceptable-action severity, autonomy violations.

Uncertainty

Expected calibration error, Brier score, confidence/coverage curves and selective intervention.

Personalization

Same opportunity state, different user boundaries. The intervention should change when user preferences change.

Generalization

Separate OOD-domain and OOD-trigger protocols expose where the structured learned policy stops generalizing.

Scientific boundary

These are synthetic-oracle experiments. They are appropriate for controlled mechanism tests, regression infrastructure and ablations. They are not evidence that the oracle reflects real users. Human annotation, external-model evaluation and longitudinal deployment remain explicitly unclaimed.
